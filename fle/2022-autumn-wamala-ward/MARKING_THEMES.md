# Marking themes

Own-words notes on common gaps, drawn from the examiner's report for this sitting (private copy). These are not official wording, not a mark scheme, and not a model answer. Do not paste official report or question text here.

## Paper shape

Five questions. Unattributed pasted text appeared in this sitting and was flagged as a concern.

## Recurring gaps

- Failed papers often showed thin syllabus knowledge, especially on procurement, management, and leadership.
- Ethical issues in the sustainability question were sometimes omitted altogether.
- Time ran out on later questions when early answers were long and unstructured.

## By question

- Q1: Weaker work did not go far enough on risks and opportunities, including ones not already spelled out in the scenario, and showed little procurement or leadership method.
- Q2: Naming sustainability goals without depth, and skipping conflicting ethical issues, were typical gaps.
- Q3: Some answers did not treat the outbreak as a health-and-safety management problem, or pasted an unrelated procedure without explaining how it would be used here.
- Q4: Not every limb was answered. Poor time planning often showed up on this question.
- Q5: Short, superficial ends were common. Few answers asked why performance had already slipped before proposing a recovery.
