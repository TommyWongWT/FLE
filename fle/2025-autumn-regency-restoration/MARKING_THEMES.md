# Marking themes

Own-words notes on common gaps, drawn from the examiner's report for this sitting (private copy). These are not official wording, not a mark scheme, and not a model answer. Do not paste official report or question text here.

## Paper shape

Five questions.

## Recurring gaps

- Many candidates appeared to sit too early: Part A showed limited experience, and Part B showed thin syllabus knowledge.
- Generic textbook quotations, and pasted text prepared in advance, were poor substitutes for an answer in this setting.
- Answers must be written in the case-study context, not as a general essay.

## By question

- Q1: Procurement and client oversight were often under-developed for a project with reputational sensitivity.
- Q2: Stakeholder work needs a method for competing needs, not a list of organisations.
- Q3: A common trap was to write mainly about volunteers and miss the wider health, safety, and welfare duty to everyone on the works.
- Q4: This is a project-appraisal and financial-control question in a constrained, sensitive site — not a generic cost-cutting note.
- Q5: Listing sustainability goals was a weaker pattern than showing how construction impacts would be limited and how outcomes would be achieved in this landscape.
