# Marking themes

Own-words notes on common gaps, drawn from the examiner's report for this sitting (private copy). These are not official wording, not a mark scheme, and not a model answer. Do not paste official report or question text here.

## Paper shape

Five questions.

## Recurring gaps

- Scripts that only described theory, without applying it to this link, were a common fail pattern.
- A sentence or two about selected sustainability goals was not enough for a pass on the sustainability question.
- Many answers did not distinguish management from leadership.
- Collusion, plagiarism, and use of extra project information found outside the paper led to fails. Do not search the live project during the exam.
- Unattributed pasted text and poor time management appear again in the poor-features list.

## By question

- Q1: A structured scan of risks across construction, off-site manufacture, and shipping worked better than a narrow site-only list.
- Q2: Go past a short sustainability-goal catalogue; show benefits for the communities and environment actually affected.
- Q3: Separate management and leadership, and deal with the duration and the stakeholder spread.
- Q4: Health, safety, and welfare need a through-life approach, not only a construction-phase note.
- Q5: Programme and cost shocks have to be answered as a recovery and control problem, applied to this project.
