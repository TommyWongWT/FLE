# Marking themes

Own-words notes on common gaps, drawn from the examiner's report for this sitting (private copy). These are not official wording, not a mark scheme, and not a model answer. Do not paste official report or question text here.

## Paper shape

Five questions.

## Recurring gaps

- The most common feedback on failed papers was a failure to show further learning across the syllabus.
- Pasted blocks of text, and plagiarism or cheating, led to fails (including discounted Part A marks where Part B was compromised).
- A short sustainability-goal list was not enough where a question asked about sustainability in this scheme.
- Many answers did not distinguish management from leadership.

## By question

- Q1: Feasibility-stage management answers were often thin on the knowledge expected for that role and stage.
- Q2: Stronger work compared procurement options before recommending one for these works.
- Q3: Management and leadership were frequently blurred; the construction-stage challenges need both, clearly separated.
- Q4: Health-and-safety duties of the named roles have to be specific to this project, not a generic duty list.
- Q5: Governance and sustainability both need to be answered in this context; a token sustainability paragraph was a reported weak pattern.
