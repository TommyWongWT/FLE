# Marking themes

Own-words notes on common gaps, drawn from the examiner's report for this sitting (private copy). These are not official wording, not a mark scheme, and not a model answer. Do not paste official report or question text here.

## Paper shape

Six questions. Several have more than one limb. Time pressure on the last questions was a frequent problem.

## Recurring gaps

- Many scripts missed one or more limbs.
- Late questions were often short because candidates worked strictly from Q1 to Q6 without a time plan.
- Generic tools were named without showing how they would work on this island project.

## By question

- Q1: Weaker answers did not separate internal governance from external context, skipped audit trails, or mentioned sustainability goals without discussing moral conflicts.
- Q2: Cost tools appeared without a link to longer-term growth. Social value was sometimes replaced by a generic stakeholder note.
- Q3: Risks were listed at a high level, without impacts, phases, or a way to prioritise them. Some answers looked generic or unfinished.
- Q4: Constraints were recognised, but few answers showed how to manage the programme or the longer-term benefit of local supply choices.
- Q5: Answers drifted into general safety management and missed the health-plan focus, including the effect of illness on productivity.
- Q6: Candidates who left this until last often ran out of depth.
