# Marking themes

Own-words notes on common gaps, drawn from the examiner's report for this sitting (private copy). These are not official wording, not a mark scheme, and not a model answer. Do not paste official report or question text here.

## Paper shape

Five questions, each with more than one limb.

## Recurring gaps

- Unsuccessful scripts often skipped a limb on one or more questions.
- Length was uneven: a long answer on one question and a thin answer on another.
- Cut-and-paste sustainability-goal lists added little when they were not applied to this station.

## By question

- Q1: Stronger work tied the client’s vision to procurement and collaboration. Weaker work did not connect those parts.
- Q2: The budget and specification limbs needed a joined-up commercial response, not a generic procurement essay.
- Q3: The incident question rewarded structured risk analysis. Incomplete treatment of the safety plan was a gap.
- Q4: Pasted sustainability-goal descriptions were a common weak pattern; the need was to translate objectives into a practical approach.
- Q5: Weaker answers treated stakeholder groups and conflicting needs as two disconnected notes.
