## Format and TS rules (verified, with gaps)

From the [Further Learning Exam](https://www.ice.org.uk/your-career/professional-qualification-support/getting-professionally-qualified/further-learning-exam) page:

- The exam is completed **online**.
- You have **6 hours to answer 5 questions** linked to the case scenario, plus **an extra hour for breaks**.
- You must upload the TS **three weeks before** the exam. **Failure to upload by the confirmed date means the application is automatically deferred** to the next sitting.

The 5 March 2027 upload deadline on the key-dates page is the confirmed date to follow. Three weeks before 23 March 2027 would be 2 March 2027, which is earlier than that listed deadline, so use 5 March 2027 unless ICE tells you otherwise.

**TS length (400–600 words, about 500):** not stated on either of the two web pages above. It is stated in ICE's Further Learning Exam Guidance (the official guidance document linked from the FLE page): the TS should be about 500 words, and not fewer than 400 or more than 600. Treat this as verified from that guidance, and re-check the current guidance before you write or upload.

Other wording differences, for awareness:

- The FLE page also says results are issued in **no more than 12 weeks**, and later on the same page **approximately 10 weeks**, after the exam. 13 July 2027 is about 16 weeks after 23 March 2027. Follow the published results date on the key-dates page.
- The official guidance document describes a seven-hour sitting including the break, which matches 6 hours plus 1 hour of breaks, and it describes deferral if the TS is not uploaded by the given date.
