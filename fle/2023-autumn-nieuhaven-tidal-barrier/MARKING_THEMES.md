# Marking themes

Own-words notes on common gaps, drawn from the examiner's report for this sitting (private copy). These are not official wording, not a mark scheme, and not a model answer. Do not paste official report or question text here.

## Paper shape

Five questions. The report body emphasises general strengths and weaknesses more than a long per-question commentary.

## Recurring gaps

- Failed papers often showed missing syllabus knowledge and weak preparation.
- Poor features called out in the report include unattributed pasted text, contract or sustainability-goal extracts that do not answer the question, shallow critical thinking, missing references, and poor time management.
- Resitting too quickly after a fail, without closing knowledge gaps, was discouraged.

## Question pointers

- Apply every answer to this barrier scheme, including access, phasing, and multiple contracts where the question raises them.
- The last question’s incident is set for the exam; do not spend sitting time searching for a real event.
- Balance cost and sustainability without a pasted list of goals that never touches this works package.
