# Marking themes

Own-words notes on common gaps, drawn from the examiner's report for this sitting (private copy). These are not official wording, not a mark scheme, and not a model answer. Do not paste official report or question text here.

## Paper shape

Five questions. The report body has less question-by-question commentary than later sittings and points script markers to model answer plans. Themes below come from the general comments and the limited question remarks that are there.

## Recurring gaps

- A lack of syllabus coverage showed in many failed papers.
- Long, unstructured paragraphs led to missed limbs and rushed endings.
- Recycled wording from an earlier paper, without answering this question, was a reported problem.
- Unattributed pasted text had already been a fail reason in a previous sitting; the same warning applies.

## Question pointers
