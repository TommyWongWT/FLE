# Marking themes

Own-words notes on common gaps, drawn from the examiner's report for this sitting (private copy). These are not official wording, not a mark scheme, and not a model answer. Do not paste official report or question text here.

## Paper shape

Five questions. The same good/poor features table used in neighbouring sittings applies here.

## Recurring gaps

- Unstructured essays missed limbs and ran out of time.
- Pasted contract or sustainability-goal text that was not applied to this reconstruction added little.
- Weak syllabus knowledge and shallow critical thinking showed in failed papers.
- Recycled answers from a previous paper, without answering the question actually set, were a problem.
- Part A weakness often carried into Part B; taking the exam before the learning is in place was a theme.

## By question

- Q1: Show how early contractor involvement changes procurement and the form of contract on this scheme, not a generic ECI definition.
- Q2: Health-and-safety risks need to be identified at the preliminary-design stage and then managed, not listed in the abstract.
- Q3: Explain how different parties contribute to the project’s objectives, rather than describing organisations in isolation.
- Q4: Sustainability has to be discussed for this demolition and reconstruction, not as a free-standing essay.
- Q5: The late commercial shock is a scenario device. Apply contract and project-control thinking; do not spend the exam checking whether it happened on a real site.
